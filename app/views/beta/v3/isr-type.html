Why does Ben Dormund need CAS2 accommodation?

When a person is at risk of being recalled to prison due to losing their community accommodation.
This is known as Homeless at Conditional Release Date (HCRD).
When a person has been sent back to custody for a period of time and may be homeless on re-release.
When a person has a court order requiring accommodation with high levels of monitoring and rehabilitation.
When a person is considered for early re-release from prison without referral to a parole board.
When a person is leaving Approved Premises, but still needs accommodation support.
